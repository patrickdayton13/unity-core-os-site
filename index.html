Why Unity is different

Not another portal bolted onto the old way.

Most staffing tools digitize one seat at the table. Unity Core OS replaces the table — a single system of record every party shares.

One real-time data layer

Client, vendor, and provider portals read and write the same live data. No middleware, no nightly exports, no reconciliation spreadsheets.

A neutral marketplace

Every agency bids on the same terms, in real time. Facilities award on merit and price — not on who called first.

Portable credentials

Providers maintain one verified profile. It follows them to every facility and agency — so credentialing stops starting from zero.

Nationwide intelligence

Grounded in continuously refreshed national provider, facility, and wage datasets — so matching and rates start from ground truth.

Enterprise-grade cloud

Built on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure with multi-tenant isolation — the reliability and security posture health systems require.

Free for providers

The people doing the work never pay to be found. Free forever for clinicians — on web and mobile.

How it changes the market

From a chain of hand-offs to a single shared system.

The old model loses time and truth at every boundary. Unity Core OS removes the boundaries — the request, the bid, the credential, and the invoice all live in one place, seen by everyone at once.

The old way
  • Shifts phoned and emailed to one agency at a time
  • Rates set behind closed doors, not by competition
  • Credentials re-collected and re-verified for every placement
  • Timesheets and invoices reconciled by hand, days later
The Unity way
  • One post reaches every qualified agency instantly
  • Transparent, competitive bids drive fair market rates
  • Verify a credential once — reuse it everywhere
  • Timesheet to invoice on the same live record — no re-keying
Built to be trusted

Enterprise infrastructure, healthcare-grade discipline.

Multi-tenant isolation

Every client, vendor, and provider organization is strictly scoped — data is never co-mingled across tenants.

OAuth2 everywhere

Every data request is authenticated and authorized — no anonymous access to sensitive records.

Role-based access

People see exactly what their role allows — read-only where it should be, editable where it counts.

Real-time audit trail

Every request, bid, approval, and credential change is captured on one live record.
